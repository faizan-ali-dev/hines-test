Europe Overview

Hines has been actively investing, developing and acquiring real estate in Europe for more than 30 years. Headquartered in London, Hines Europe operates from 16 offices across Denmark, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Poland, Spain, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom. The firm’s European platform includes portfolios of prominent office, retail, industrial and residential properties.
