Americas Overview

Since 1957, Hines has been extending the breadth of its experience from office projects to land development, arts and cultural, mixed-use, multifamily, sports, and medical facilities across the Americas. Headquartered in Houston, Hines Americas has a presence in 224 cities in Brazil, Canada, Mexico, Panama, Costa Rica, and The United States.
